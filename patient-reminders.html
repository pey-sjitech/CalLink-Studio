Single event

1. Connect

Emails are sent from your own Google account by a small script you set up once.

2. Import appointments

Upload a CSV (or paste rows) with the columns name, email, date, time and optionally location. Dates as YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY; times as 14:30 or 2:30 PM.

Don't include diagnoses, treatments or other medical details. Emails contain only name, date, time and place.

3. Review and send

PatientEmailAppointmentRemindersStatus

Each patient gets a confirmation now, then a reminder 1 week and 1 day before. Appointments closer than that skip the reminders that no longer fit. To cancel one, set its Status to CANCELLED in the Google Sheet.

Patient details are sent only to your own Google script. This page stores nothing about patients.

This emails each patient a confirmation right now and schedules their reminders. It cannot be undone from this page.

One-time setup (about 10 minutes)

  1. Create a new Google Sheet. Open Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Press , delete the sample code in the editor, paste, and save.
  3. Open Project Settings → Script Properties and add SECRET with a long random value (12+ characters). Keep it private.
  4. Choose Deploy → New deployment → Web app. Execute as Me, access Anyone. Authorise when asked, then copy the web app URL.
  5. Set the script's time zone in Project Settings to your clinic's.
  6. Paste the URL and secret above, then press Test connection.

Patient data is kept in the "Appointments" sheet of your own spreadsheet and deleted 30 days after each appointment.
